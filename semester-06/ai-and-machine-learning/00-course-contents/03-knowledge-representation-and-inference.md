## Unit 3: Knowledge Representation and Inference (12 Hours)

### 3.1 Knowledge Representation

* Knowledge Base
* Knowledge Representation
* Production-Based Systems
* Frame-Based Systems

### 3.2 Inference Mechanisms

* Introduction to Inference
* Forward Chaining
* Backward Chaining

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the role of knowledge representation in Artificial Intelligence.
* Develop knowledge bases using production rules and frame-based systems.
* Apply forward and backward chaining for logical inference.
* Analyze and compare different knowledge representation techniques.
* Design simple rule-based expert systems using inference mechanisms.
