* Divisive Clustering

### 5.3 Soft Clustering

* Fuzzy Clustering

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the principles of unsupervised learning.
* Apply K-means and hierarchical clustering algorithms to group data.
* Distinguish between agglomerative and divisive clustering approaches.
* Describe fuzzy clustering and its advantages over hard clustering.
* Evaluate clustering techniques for different data analysis applications.
