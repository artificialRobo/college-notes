## Unit 2: Top-Down Parsing (12 Hours)

### 2.1 Introduction to Parsing

* Role of the Parser
* Error Handling in Parsing
* Context-Free Grammars (CFGs)
* Writing Context-Free Grammars

### 2.2 Grammar Transformations

* Elimination of Ambiguity
* Elimination of Left Recursion
* Left Factoring

### 2.3 Top-Down Parsing Techniques

* Recursive Descent Parser
* Predictive Parser
* LL(1) Parser

### 2.4 Construction of LL(1) Parsers

* Computation of FIRST Sets
* Computation of FOLLOW Sets
* Construction of Predictive Parsing Table
* LL(1) Grammars
* Predictive Parsing Algorithm

### 2.5 Error Recovery

* Problems Related to Predictive Parsing
* Error Recovery in Predictive Parsing

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the role of parsing in compiler design.
* Construct and transform context-free grammars suitable for top-down parsing.
* Compute FIRST and FOLLOW sets.
* Develop LL(1) parsing tables and implement predictive parsing.
* Apply error detection and recovery techniques in predictive parsers.
