**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the principles of bottom-up parsing techniques.
* Construct and analyze shift-reduce and operator precedence parsers.
* Build SLR, Canonical LR(1), and LALR parsing tables.
* Compare different LR parsing methods and identify their applications.
* Use parser generation tools such as YACC.
