## Unit 1: Introduction to Compiler Design (12 Hours)

### 1.1 Introduction to Compilers

* Definition and Functions of a Compiler
* Phases of a Compiler
* Cousins of the Compiler
* Grouping of Compiler Phases
* Compiler Construction Tools

### 1.2 Lexical Analysis

* Role of the Lexical Analyzer
* Input Buffering Techniques
* Specification of Tokens
* Lexical Analyzer Generator (LEX)

### 1.3 Finite Automata and Token Recognition

* Finite Automata (FA)
* Regular Expressions
* Conversion of Regular Expressions to Finite Automata
* Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA)
* DFA Minimization

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the overall structure and phases of a compiler.
* Describe the role of lexical analysis in compilation.
* Design token specifications using regular expressions.
* Convert regular expressions into finite automata.
* Minimize deterministic finite automata for efficient lexical analysis.
