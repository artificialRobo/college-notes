* Directional Smoothing

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Apply gray-level transformation techniques to enhance images.
* Perform histogram equalization for contrast enhancement.
* Explain convolution and template-based image processing.
* Apply spatial filtering and window-based operations.
* Compare different image smoothing techniques.
