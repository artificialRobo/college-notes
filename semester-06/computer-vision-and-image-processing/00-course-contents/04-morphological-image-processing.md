**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the principles of mathematical morphology in image processing.
* Apply basic morphological operations for image enhancement.
* Perform opening and closing operations to remove noise and improve image quality.
* Use area-based operations for image analysis.
* Apply morphological transformations in practical image processing applications.
