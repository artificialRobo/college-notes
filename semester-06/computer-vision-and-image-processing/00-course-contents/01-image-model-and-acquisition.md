## Unit 1: Image Model and Image Acquisition (12 Hours)

### 1.1 Introduction to Digital Images

* Image Model
* Image Shape
* Sampling

### 1.2 Types of Digital Images

* Intensity Images
* Color Images
* Range Images

### 1.3 Image Acquisition

* Image Capture
* Image Scanners

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the digital image formation process and image model.
* Describe different types of digital images.
* Understand image sampling and representation techniques.
* Explain various methods of image acquisition.
* Compare image capture devices and scanning technologies.
