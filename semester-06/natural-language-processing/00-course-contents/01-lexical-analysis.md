## Unit 1: Lexical Analysis (9 Hours)

### 1.1 Lexical Analysis

* Introduction to Lexical Analysis
* Regular Expressions and Automata for String Matching

### 1.2 Morphology

* Words and Word Forms
* Fundamentals of Morphology
* Morphological Diversity of Indian Languages
* Morphology Paradigms
* Finite State Machine (FSM) and Finite State Transducer (FST)-Based Morphology
* Automatic Morphology Learning

### 1.3 Part-of-Speech Tagging and Language Modeling

* Parts of Speech (POS)
* N-gram Language Models
* Hidden Markov Models (HMM)

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the concepts of lexical analysis in natural language processing.
* Apply regular expressions and finite automata for string matching.
* Describe morphological analysis techniques and their applications.
* Explain the morphological characteristics of Indian languages.
* Apply N-gram models and Hidden Markov Models for language processing tasks.
