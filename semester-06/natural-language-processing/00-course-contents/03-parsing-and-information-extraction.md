## Unit 3: Parsing and Information Extraction (9 Hours)

### 3.1 Parsing Fundamentals

* Theories of Parsing
* Parsing Algorithms

### 3.2 Parsing Techniques

* Earley Parser
* Cocke–Younger–Kasami (CYK) Parser
* Probabilistic Parsing
* Resolving Attachment and Structural Ambiguity
* Shallow Parsing
* Dependency Parsing

### 3.3 Information Extraction

* Named Entity Recognition (NER)
* Maximum Entropy Models
* Conditional Random Fields (CRFs)

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the theoretical foundations of parsing in natural language processing.
* Apply Earley, CYK, and probabilistic parsing techniques.
* Analyze and resolve syntactic ambiguities in natural language.
* Perform shallow parsing, dependency parsing, and named entity recognition.
* Explain the use of Maximum Entropy Models and Conditional Random Fields for sequence labeling tasks.
