## Unit 4: Lexical Knowledge Networks (9 Hours)

### 4.1 Lexical Knowledge Networks

* Meaning and Lexical Knowledge Networks
* WordNet Theory
* Indian Language WordNets
* Multilingual Dictionaries

### 4.2 Semantic Analysis

* Semantic Roles
* Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD)
* WSD and Multilinguality

### 4.3 Discourse Processing

* Metaphors
* Coreference Resolution
* Anaphora Resolution

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the role of lexical knowledge networks in natural language processing.
* Describe the structure and applications of WordNet and multilingual lexical resources.
* Apply semantic role analysis and word sense disambiguation techniques.
* Explain multilingual aspects of semantic processing.
* Analyze discourse using metaphor interpretation, coreference resolution, and anaphora resolution.
