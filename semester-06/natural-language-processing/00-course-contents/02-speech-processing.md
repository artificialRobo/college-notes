## Unit 2: Speech Processing (9 Hours)

### 2.1 Fundamentals of Speech Processing

* Biology of Speech Processing
* Place and Manner of Articulation
* Word Boundary Detection

### 2.2 Automatic Speech Recognition

* Argmax-Based Computations
* Hidden Markov Models for Speech Recognition

### 2.3 Text-to-Speech Synthesis

* Introduction to Text-to-Speech (TTS)
* Rule-Based Approach
* Concatenative-Based Approach

**Learning Outcomes**
After completing this unit, students will be able to:

* Explain the biological and linguistic foundations of speech processing.
* Describe speech production mechanisms and articulation.
* Apply Hidden Markov Models in speech recognition systems.
* Explain the role of argmax computations in speech recognition.
* Compare rule-based and concatenative approaches to text-to-speech synthesis.
